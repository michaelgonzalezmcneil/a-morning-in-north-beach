Bella is my long-haired Chihuahua. She joins me here in an imagined morning walk; her presence in the game does not mean she accompanied every real visit.

My usual kind of pause

I visited Caffè Trieste during my SF internship summer. My memory here is ordering a matcha latte. This little cup is a nod to that visit—a personal recollection, rather than a promise about today’s menu.

A note about this little world

This is a stylized interpretation with compressed walking distances. Interiors are invented. Vesuvio’s morning access is game fiction. Bella’s honey-and-cream coat is provisional.

All game artwork is original and generated locally. Geographic references: Caffè Trieste, City Lights, and SF Recreation & Parks.
